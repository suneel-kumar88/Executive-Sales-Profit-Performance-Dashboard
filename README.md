# Executive Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI executive dashboard designed to track overall revenue, profitability margins, product portfolio performance, sales rep contributions, and regional distribution.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

![Executive Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard] https://github.com/suneel-kumar88/Executive-Sales-Profit-Performance-Dashboard/blob/757a3cecf63014601932cd0955df68310b43c780/Executive%20Sales%20Dashboard11.png

This dashboard provides sales leadership, commercial directors, and business analysts with high-level and granular visibility into target attainment, revenue channels, top product lines, and sales team productivity.

## 📊 Visual Breakdown & Insights

### 1. Target Tracking & Revenue Trends
* **Sales and Monthly Target Comparison:** Gauge visualization tracking actual revenue (42M) against a 67M monthly target, within an 810 to 85M scale.
* **Monthly Revenue and Profit Trend:** Dual-line graph illustrating concurrent revenue and profit movement throughout the year, with peak surges observed in May and July.

### 2. Category & Product Line Contribution
* **Revenue by Product Category:**
  * **Electronics:** 27M

  * **Furniture:** 3M

### 3. Geographic & Sales Channel Performance

  * **South:** 8.0M Revenue | 2.4M Profit
* **Total Revenue by Channel:** Direct channel accounts for 49%, Dealer represents 33%, and Online contributes 18%.

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters

* **Hierarchy Slicers:** `Product`, `SubCategory`, `State`, `Region`, `City`, and `Salesperson` multi-select dropdowns.
* **Customer Search:** Interactive input box to look up specific individual accounts.
* **Date Slicer:** Multi-period calendar filter for historical timeline analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Power Query ( dimensional modeling, and schema relationships)
* **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for Profit Margins, Target Gauges, Channel Splits, and Revenue Totals
* **UI/UX Design:** High-contrast executive light/dark hybrid layout with color-coded KPI cards and progress gauges
